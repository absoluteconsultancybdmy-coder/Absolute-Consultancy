# Marketing Plan: Absolute Consultancy Firm Partnership Proposal

## Executive Summary

Absolute Consultancy Firm presents a comprehensive partnership opportunity for universities seeking to increase international student enrollment from Bangladesh and Malaysia. With a proven track record of 99% visa approval rates and personalized end-to-end support, we connect qualified students with institutions that match their academic goals and financial capabilities.

## 1. Market Analysis

### Target Markets
- **Bangladesh**: Growing middle class seeking quality international education
- **Malaysia**: Domestic students looking for private university options
- **Secondary Markets**: Other ASEAN countries through our expanding network

### Student Demographics
- Age range: 17-24 years
- Academic interests: Business, Engineering, Medicine, IT, Hospitality, Law
- Budget-conscious but value quality education
- Prefer guided application processes with high success rates

### Competitive Advantages
- 99% visa approval rate (industry-leading)
- Personalized COO involvement in every case
- Free initial consultations
- Comprehensive scholarship guidance
- Strong university partnerships (30+ institutions)

## 2. Services Overview

### Core Services for Universities
1. **Student Recruitment & Screening**
   - Pre-qualification of applicants based on academic merit and financial capacity
   - Cultural and academic preparedness assessment
   - Document verification and authentication

2. **Application Management**
   - End-to-end application submission support
   - SOP and personal statement refinement
   - Application status tracking and follow-up

3. **Visa Processing Assistance**
   - Document preparation and verification
   - Interview preparation and mock sessions
   - Liaison with immigration authorities

4. **Scholarship & Financial Aid Support**
   - Identification of eligible scholarships and bursaries
   - Application assistance for financial aid programs
   - Guidance on education loans and payment plans

5. **Pre-Departure & Arrival Support**
   - Accommodation arrangements
   - Cultural orientation programs
   - Airport pickup and settling-in assistance
