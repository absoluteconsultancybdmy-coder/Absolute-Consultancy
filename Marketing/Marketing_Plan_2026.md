## 4. CHANNEL STRATEGY

### 4.1 Website (absoluteconsultancyfirm.com) — Foundation asset
- **SEO cluster model**: 5 pillar pages (3,000+ words each) →
  1. "Study in Malaysia: The Complete 2026 Guide"
  2. "Malaysia Student Visa: Step-by-Step Approval Guide"
  3. "Affordable Universities in Malaysia for Bangladeshi Students"
  4. "Scholarships in Malaysia: Fully-Funded Options for 2026"
  5. "Foundation, Diploma, Bachelor, Master, PhD: Which Path is Right?"
- Each pillar = 10–20 cluster blog posts (e.g. "Best universities in KL for Computer Science")
- Publish **1 blog/week**. Target long-tail keywords ("study in malaysia for bangladeshi students 2026")
- Add an **FAQ schema** block + **course schema** for each university page → boost Google rich results
- **Add Live Chat / WhatsApp widget** in bottom-right (free via Tidio or Chatra) — 3× more conversions than contact form alone
- Already done: clean React site, 35 unis, fast, SEO meta tags, sitemap.xml, robots.txt ✅

### 4.2 Facebook — Primary lead-gen channel
- **Frequency**: 5 posts/week + daily stories
- **Content mix** (proven highest-converting):
  - **35%** Student success stories (real photos, real names, "Arisha got into MMU in 2 weeks")
  - **25%** Educational (visa checklists, scholarship deadlines, IELTS tips)
  - **20%** University highlights (campus tour clips, programme reels)
  - **10%** Behind-the-scenes (the COO at work, "day in the life of a counsellor")
  - **10%** Promotional (free consultation, "Apply before Feb 28 intake")
- **Ad strategy** (RM 500–800/month):
  - Lead-form ads targeting: 18–30, Bangladesh/Malaysia/India/Nigeria, interests in "study abroad", "IELTS", specific university names
  - **3 ad sets**: Awareness (video views) · Consideration (university guides download) · Conversion (consultation booking)
  - Retarget website visitors with "Still thinking about studying in Malaysia? Book a free 15-min call"
- **Use Facebook groups** (study-abroad groups in Bangladesh/India) — not to spam, but to genuinely answer questions and build authority. COO should comment weekly.
- **Goal**: 30 leads/month from FB ads at RM 15–25 cost-per-lead

### 4.3 YouTube (@absoluteconsultancy) — Trust builder
- **Long-form (1 video/week, 8–12 min)**:
  - "Day in the life of a Bangladeshi student at Taylor's University"
  - "How we got a 99% visa approval rate (case studies)"
  - "Malaysia vs Bangladesh: Cost, Quality, Career Opportunities"
  - "How to write a winning SOP (full walkthrough)"
  - "Inside MMU campus tour" (use existing campus videos from university pages)
- **Shorts (3/week, 30–60 sec)**:
  - Visa myth busters ("You don't need RM 100k in your bank — here's the real amount")
  - Quick scholarship tips
  - 1-question student Q&A
- **SEO for YouTube**: title = "How to [X] in Malaysia 2026" · tags = "study in malaysia, malaysia student visa, [university name]" · embed in blog posts
- **Goal**: 1,000 subs in 6 months, 50k views/month

### 4.4 Instagram / Reels / TikTok — Reach multiplier
- **Reels**: 3/week — take the best YouTube Shorts and re-cut for IG/TikTok
- Use trending audio, captions in English + Bangla
- Repurpose every YouTube long-form into 3–5 short clips
- **Goal**: 1k followers in 90 days

### 4.5 LinkedIn — B2B + executive branding
- **COO as the face** of the brand (this is huge for trust)
- Post 2–3×/week:
  - "3 reasons Bangladeshi students pick Malaysia over the UK"
  - "What I learned counselling 300+ students this year"
  - Repost student testimonials
- Connect with university partnership managers, education agents
- **Goal**: Position COO as Malaysia's go-to study-abroad expert

### 4.6 WhatsApp — Conversion channel
- Already integrated as the "Apply via WhatsApp" CTA ✅
- **Upgrade to WhatsApp Business app** (free):
  - Catalogue of all 35 universities with photos
  - Automated greeting: "Hi! Welcome to Absolute Consultancy. Reply with 1 for universities, 2 for visa, 3 for scholarships"
  - Quick replies for common questions
- **WhatsApp Status**: Post 3×/week (university highlights, student wins) — same content as IG Stories

### 4.7 Email / SMS — Nurture (Day 30+)
- Free tool: **Mailchimp free tier** (500 contacts) or **Brevo** (300 emails/day free)
- **5-email drip sequence** for new leads:
  1. Day 0: "Thanks for your interest — here's your free Malaysia Study Guide (PDF)"
  2. Day 3: "3 students who just got into [university] this month"
  3. Day 7: "Free webinar: How to get a 99% visa approval"
  4. Day 14: "Scholarship deadline closing in 2 weeks — apply now"
  5. Day 30: "Still thinking? Book a free 15-min call with our COO"

## 7. PARTNERSHIPS & GROWTH HACKS

1. **Alumni ambassadors**: Pay RM 100–300 commission per referral to placed students in KL/Cyberjaya. They post on FB groups and tag you.
2. **Education fairs**: Attend 1 Bangladesh fair per quarter (IDP, AECC, your own booth) — collect leads for the WhatsApp drip.
3. **University partnership pages**: Ask each of 35 partner unis to feature you on their "official agents" page (free backlinks + authority).
4. **Google Business Profile**: Set up for your KL office → show up on Maps for "study abroad consultant Kuala Lumpur".
5. **Referral bonus for students**: "Refer a friend, get RM 200 cash / RM 200 fee discount when they enroll".
